    {#- The trajectory-specific controls: the FORCE PARAMETERS. What this page hands MolView is RAW per-frame forces (``molview.data.setForces``), never a finished appearance: MolView's engine builds and styles the arrows from them, with the largest force highlighted (web/molview.md § 9.2 — "there is no set the arrows"; § 9.3 for the door). Whether they are DRAWN is MolView's single "show overlay" view-toggle (the overlay IS the force vectors); there is no show/hide control here. These knobs only shape HOW the arrows are computed — length scale, magnitude threshold, and whether frozen-atom arrows are excluded. Changing any of them regenerates the arrows and MolView redraws them in place; the overlay's visibility is unchanged. -#}
    Overlay force vectors {#- "Exclude frozen atoms" affects the force arrows only: frozen atoms' forces are constraint-balancing artefacts, not physical free-atom forces, so their arrows are skipped.
