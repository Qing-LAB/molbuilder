Experimental — Wyckoff 1963 room-T value. Use when matching crystallographic geometry or running LDA / HSE.

PBE (all-electron) — Haas, Tran, Blaha 2009 PRB 79 085104. Use when your device run is PBE-XC; avoids the ~1-2% lead/device lattice mismatch that experimental introduces.

Your bulk run — measured in YOUR SIESTA + PSML setup via a bulk-cell relax. Most accurate when populated. Greyed if not yet measured. All-electron PBE and PSML PBE can differ by 0.5-1.5%.

Ni note: the PBE value assumes SpinPolarized=T (ferromagnetic ground state).
