How the box is decided

By default the box is derived: it is the structure's extent plus the vacuum you set on each side, centred on the molecule. If you set an explicit unit cell, the box switches to manual — the cell is then what it says, and the vacuum numbers only tell you what the gaps happen to be.

Every change here is checked by the backend before it is applied, and it may be corrected or refused: cells must be right-handed, and along a non-periodic direction the box must contain every atom (along a periodic direction, atoms outside are legitimate periodic images). Whenever no origin of your own is set, the corner is derived so the box wraps the structure — it never jumps to (0, 0, 0). Anything the check has to say appears in the message bar at the top of the page.
