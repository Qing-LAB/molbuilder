Transport calculation

Generates TranSIESTA (NEGF) and PySCF-NEGF input scripts for a relaxed device geometry. Pick the relaxed structure in the Projects sidebar (double-click to commit); the form below is populated from TransportConfig via the schema endpoint /api/transport/schema.

Where chemistry comes from on Transport. TransportConfig intentionally has no (charge, spin, method) fields — those travel from the upstream relaxed geometry via its .molstruct.json sidecar. Set them once on Optimization or Spectrum calculation (the Auto-detect button on either page surfaces the chemistry rationale before you generate), commit the result, then load the relaxed structure here. When the TranSIESTA / PySCF-NEGF engines land (Phase B.3), the form will also gain an Auto-detect step that consumes the same ChemistryAnalysis middle layer (see docs/protocols/scientific-validation.md § 2.5).
