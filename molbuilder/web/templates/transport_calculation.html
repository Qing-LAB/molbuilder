1. The junction

Transport is an assembly built from ONE cited directory, and what makes a directory citable is its files — never its name or where it sits: a finished relaxation (.fdf + .XV together; the deck is the electronic contract) or a labeled structure (.xyz + .molstruct.json; the contract fields open up in Parameters, since there is no deck to be truth). Pick any directory — the meta line classifies it and says exactly what is missing when it does not qualify. The viewer below shows the CITED labeled structure (a different bridge variation means citing a different directory). Labels are assigned where the junction is built — the Molbuilder tab — never here.
Usual convention: L-electrode low z, R-electrode high z (TranSIESTA's own — the first atoms are the −A3 lead). The atom order and the semi-infinite directions follow the geometry; µ = +V/2 follows the name. A junction labeled the other way round still runs — it biases the high-z lead instead — and is flagged here with a one-click rename.

{#- ===================== 4. Describe ===================== -#}

4. Describe

This button writes the finished task.json into the folder selected in the Projects sidebar — a folder of its own, never the cited junction (the calculation does not live inside its citation; make one under this project's transport topic and select it). Nothing is left to ask: the five stages and the shape are fixed by design, and prep derives everything else from the citation above (the sorted copy, both electrode cells, the seed, the NEGF device, the TBtrans transmission). The electronic contract (basis, XC, mesh) comes from the cited attempt's own .fdf; form fields you changed travel as stage overrides where they are transport's own (transmission window, contour). More than one voltage is a scan: one attempt ladder per point, launched as one chain job.

{#- The road after describing — the composite's own quickstart. -#}
After describing: the five-stage road
  1. task.json is the whole floor 2 (no template: the contract arrives from the citation at prep). Prep from Task setup's buttons on that folder, or the CLI.
  2. prep run seed → launch; then electrode_L, electrode_R; then device (a bias scan launches as ONE chain job walking the points, each warm-started from the previous point's .TSDE); then transmission.
  3. summarize run — writes <label>.transport.json (T(E) per bias, G(E_F), the I–V table from TBtrans's own integral).
