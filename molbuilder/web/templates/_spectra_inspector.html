Mode visualisation

The mode shape (which atoms move, in what direction, relative amplitudes) is faithful from the diagonalisation. The animation amplitude is a visualisation knob, not a physical quantity -- thermal RMS amplitudes are typically <0.05 Å and too small to see, so this slider exaggerates the motion the way Gaussian / Jmol / Avogadro do. Fixed atoms (greyed out) stay still.
