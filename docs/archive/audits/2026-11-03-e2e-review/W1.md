## 4. The pattern

The five files were last revised at 2.54.0/2.55.1 and their *assertions* mostly still hold, because Task setup's
page contract barely moved after 2.56: the drift is in **premises and citations**, not outcomes. A test step written
before the launch rows became standing rows (2026-10-11) still "adds" a row and passes because the row already stands;
a fixture written when `use_gpu` was a template value still calls its sweep a GPU sweep after run settings left the
template; an isolation fixture written before the suite's sandbox (§ 2b) now works against it and is propped up by a
hand-written calculation record. Sections were renumbered (`stages.md § 6.8d/e`, `projects.md § 4.1`) and the
citations were not followed. The rest is the older habits the brief names: page copy pinned where the contract fixes
only the fact, a calculation hand-built with `write_task` instead of described, raw sessionStorage planted in five
copies of one `_open`, and two tests that restate a neighbour.
