## 3. OWED — rules on these roads changed since 2.57.0 that only the engine or browser shows, unpinned

1. **2.65.1 — an engine's own start stop is prepared as asked and shown in its own words** (siesta.md § 6;
   parse.md § 2b `error_message`: "where that is `die`'s own *Stopping Program from Node*, its reason, the
   line before it"). A `copy_of` the layered H2 with a setting SIESTA stops on (OMM with a spin it does not
   hold, or a stated spin count on non-collinear): prep says it as info, launch runs, the run reads *failed —
   stopped before its end: <SIESTA's line>*, in `status`, the record and the Results Reason. Seconds of
   engine time.
2. **2.61.0 / job-system § 6.0a / running-a-job § 4.2 *Asked once, written once*** — on the `H2stop`
   sequence the pass already stops: the stopped group's run reads *failed — stopped before its end* (and
   `run.json` gains `ended` once), the group never reached reads *ready to launch*, `jobs status` says the
   job once. (changelog 2.61.0 lists "a sequence of groups stopped at its terminal" as not tested.)
3. **2.62.1 / checkpointing § 9** — on the `H2seq` sequence: `checkpoint list` holds, per group, `… · before
   launch task group N (<stage>), in job <host>-<pid> (sequence <seq>)` (tier 1 only plans it).
4. **2.64.0 — a SIESTA run's seconds per iteration come from its own timing file**, never the seeded
   progress log: on layered run-0, the record's s/iter equals `scf_timing_metrics` of its
   `.scf-timing.log`, non-zero.
5. **2.64.0/2.65.0 — the Results load takes the engine from the description; the run heading reads the
   engine's words and the calculation's noun** (results.md; changelog "Untested before F9"): assert both on
   the loads `test_the_run_reads_its_own_deck` and the stopped run's browser walk already make.
6. **2.63.0 — the session log's `argv:` line says what the launch passed** (running-a-job § 1 example:
   `argv: H2_01_coarse.run.sh -np 1 -omp 1`) — on layered run-0.
7. (Low) 2.63.0's untested *advice for a real propor stop* — no cheap road; 2.64.1/2.65.0 a GPU run on
   ScaLAPACK in the GPU build — needs the GPU machine; its prep half is tier 1's.
