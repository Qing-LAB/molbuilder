## Confirmed W findings

**H-D1 — modified residues placed in the lab frame.** A person who builds a phosphopeptide or a methylated/acetylated lysine gets a side chain pointing in a fixed lab direction, whatever the residue's orientation. Where the patch lands on its own residue (`[SEP]`, `AR[SEP]C`, `[PTR]`) the atoms nearly coincide: P 0.24–0.58 Å from CB or CZ. Elsewhere the bond angles are wrong (CB–OG–P 77° at best) or the contacts are 1.0–1.2 Å. Between 0.3 and 0.7 Å it is only a warning, and the run can go ahead. Smallest fix: in `_patch_residue`, express each offset in a local frame built from the anchor's bond, x along parent→anchor (CB→OG, CZ→OH, CE→NZ) with a third atom (CA, CE1, CD) fixing the plane, and rotate it into place. Also drop the H-only `remove_atoms`, or apply them after `add_hydrogens`.

**H-D3 — Z-DNA starting with C built starting with G.** A person who asks for Z-form `CGCGCG` (the panel's own example) or `ds,CGCG` gets the strand 5′-GCGCGC-3′, a different 5′ base and molecule, with no finding. Smallest fix: accept only `(GC)n` for Z in `_is_alternating_gc` and the panel's check, with the refusal saying fiber builds 5′-(GC)n. The alternative is to compare the built sequence with the asked one and say any difference.

**H-D4 — single nucleotide by AmberTools lacks HO3′.** A person who builds one nucleotide with amber (asked, or auto with no 3DNA) gets deoxyadenosine or adenosine one H short: an odd-electron radical that `add_hydrogens="auto"` skips (H/heavy 0.67), with no `build.terminal` finding. Smallest fix: in `_residue_codes_for_sequence`, give `n == 1` the both-ends suffix `N` (`DAN`, `AN`), which the loaded OL15/OL3 libs carry with both HO5′ and HO3′.

**H-D7 — build by name loses stereochemistry.** A person who builds "D-alanine", "(R)-carvone" or a steroid by name gets a structure with no stereo input. ETKDG (seed 0xF00D) picks one isomer: D-alanine comes out as L-alanine, deterministically and with no finding. Smallest fix: in `smiles_for_name`, prefer pubchempy's isomeric `smiles` (fallback `isomeric_smiles`) and keep `connectivity_smiles` only as the last resort. Fix the module docstring's "canonical SMILES" too.

**O-D6 — a person's `env_init.preamble` replaced.** A person who wrote `"env_init": {"preamble": "module load mamba/2.6.2"}` without an activation (legal), then runs init-config or bootstrap and takes "conda activate" (recommended whenever conda's hook is on disk), loses the `module load` line to the hook line. Their jobs then enter envs without the module. The step reports "added env_init.activation" and does not say a value was replaced. Smallest fix: in `seed_machine_config`'s existing-file branch, write only `activation` (and the hook preamble only when the file has no `preamble`), or report the replaced value.
